# SkillSpark AI 
### Turn Your Skill Gaps Into Career Growth

**Team:** SuperB

SkillSpark AI is an AI-powered career guidance web application that compares a user's resume with a target job description, identifies skill gaps, and generates a personalized four-week learning roadmap.

## Problem Statement

Students and freshers often struggle to identify which skills they need for their dream jobs and what they should learn next. SkillSpark AI helps connect current resume evidence with job requirements and turns the results into actionable learning steps.

## Our Solution

- **Skill Gap Analysis:** Compare resume content with job requirements.
- **Skill Classification:** Identify matched, partially demonstrated, and not-found-in-resume skills.
- **Personalized Roadmap:** Generate a four-week learning plan based on identified gaps.
- **Progress Tracking:** Track completed learning tasks and roadmap progress.

## How We Use Google Gemini

The Gemini API processes resume text and job descriptions to help identify relevant skills, explain potential gaps, and generate a tailored learning roadmap. AI-generated results are guidance and should be reviewed for accuracy.
